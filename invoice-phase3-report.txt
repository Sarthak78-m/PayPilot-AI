3. STATUS TRANSITION RULES
---------------------------

  Current Status | Allowed Next Statuses
  ---------------|---------------------------------------------
  DRAFT          | PENDING, CANCELLED
  PENDING        | CANCELLED, OVERDUE (PAID via payment processing only)
  OVERDUE        | CANCELLED (PAID via payment processing only)
  PAID           | NONE (Terminal status — immutable)
  CANCELLED      | NONE (Terminal status — immutable)

  Enforcement:
  - Ordinary invoice APIs reject any attempt by the client to directly set status to "PAID" (HTTP 400).
  - Only verified payment processing (PayPal Phase 4) may transition an invoice to PAID.
  - Invoices in PAID status cannot have any fields modified or deleted (HTTP 409).
  - Invoices in CANCELLED status cannot be edited, revived, or cancelled again (HTTP 409).


4. DELETION AND CANCELLATION BEHAVIOR
---------------------------------------

  - DRAFT Invoices: Checked against the `payments` table. If no payment records are associated, the invoice row is physically deleted from `public.invoices`. If dependent records exist, it is soft-cancelled.
  - PENDING & OVERDUE Invoices: Soft-deleted by updating `status` to `CANCELLED` and `updated_at` to current timestamp. This preserves the legal and financial audit trail.
  - PAID Invoices: Protected against deletion and cancellation (HTTP 409).
  - CANCELLED Invoices: Attempting repeated cancellation returns HTTP 409.
